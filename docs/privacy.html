Noviq. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What Noviq is

Noviq is a desktop notes application. It runs entirely on your computer. We do not operate a server, we do not collect analytics or telemetry, and we do not receive your notes.

Where your data is stored

Your pages, attachments and settings are stored as files in a folder (the "vault") on your own device. That data never leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Noviq uses the Google Drive API to copy your vault to your own Google Drive so your devices stay in step.

  • Access requested: only the drive.appdata scope, plus your email address. This gives Noviq access to its own hidden application folder in your Drive. Noviq cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Email address: used only to show you which account is connected. It is stored on your device.
  • Sign-in token: stored on your device in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain). It is not sent to us.
  • Synced content: goes directly between your device and Google. We never see or store it.

You can disconnect at any time from Noviq's settings, which removes the token from your device. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the synced copy, remove Noviq's app data from your Google Drive settings.

Noviq's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, because we do not have it.

Children

Noviq is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
