Nevis. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What Nevis is

Nevis is a desktop notes application. It runs on your computer. We do not collect analytics or telemetry. We do not receive your notes or anything else from you.

Where your data is stored

Your pages, attachments and settings are stored as files in a folder (the "vault") on your own device. That data never leaves your device.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone: we do not have it.

Children

Nevis is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
