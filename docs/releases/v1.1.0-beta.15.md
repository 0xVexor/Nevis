# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 15

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## New

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 14, plus:

- **A pages panel that works like an IDE's project view.**
  - Its tools sit in the panel's header: select the opened page (Ctrl+Alt+R), expand all, collapse all, options, and hide.
  - A click selects a page; a double-click or Enter opens it. Folders open and close with a double-click, their arrow, or the arrow keys.
  - The keyboard moves through the tree: up and down, right into a folder and left back out, Home and End, and Shift+F10 for a page's menu.
  - Type while the tree has focus to jump to a page whose title contains the letters; Esc clears.
  - Options: always select the page you open (on by default), open pages with a single click, and tree or compact view.
  - Only the selected page is highlighted; the open page shows in the accent colour.
  - The Tasks and Settings panels share the same header, with New list in Tasks.

## Fixes

- **A steadier block handle.** The six-dot handle stays with the block under the pointer, over links, table cells and checkboxes too, and no longer flickers between blocks or on the way to it. Its block is tinted only while you point at the handle or move the block, and the handle sits level with the block's first line.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and a PKG.

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
