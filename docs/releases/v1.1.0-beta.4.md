# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 4

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## What's new

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 3, plus:

- **A landscape on Home.** The greeting now sits on a minimal landscape that follows the time of day: dawn, morning, afternoon, sunset and night with a moon and stars.
- **Landscape covers.** Eleven abstract mountain and landscape covers join the cover picker. They are drawn in code, so they take no space in your vault.
- **Moving blocks.** A block is picked up by its grip beside it (larger and clearer, and clear of the text). Pressing on text or on the blank space around it always selects text.
- **Right-to-left.** In a right-to-left interface a row follows the direction of its title: arrow, icon, count and buttons mirror together. In an English interface rows keep their layout and only the title text is aligned by its own direction. Quotes, callouts and toggles follow the direction of their own text.
- **One mouse pointer.** Every pointer state (arrow, hand, text, grab, resize, zoom) now shares one minimal look.
- **Home.** Writing streak is smaller and ends level with Recent pages, which shows at most six pages. A gap above the first row of the streak calendar is fixed.
- **Window.** The window no longer shrinks below 1366 × 825.
- **Connect an AI app.** Pages made from Markdown now work (they were refused before). AI apps can only set the page icons the app ships (`list_icons`), create folders without a body, change, trash and restore database rows, add database properties, list tags and read links and backlinks. The connection waits and retries instead of failing when a client sends many requests at once. 21 tools in all.

## Under the hood

- The old layered styles and `skin.css` are gone: every stylesheet lives in `src/design-system/`, with shared `Button`, `IconButton`, `Row`, `Card`, `Switch`, `Field`, `Chip`, `Tabs`, `Section`, `Dialog` and `Tooltip` components and a development-only gallery.
- A UI guard (`npm run check:ui`) runs in CI and keeps new code on the design system.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
