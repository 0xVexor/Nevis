# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 3

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## What's new

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 2 (the calmer look, new page header and covers, back and forward, graph and database restyle), plus:

- **Persian headings.** The line under a heading 1 and the bar beside a heading 2 now sit on the right edge for right-to-left headings.
- **Tables.** Column resizing now works in right-to-left tables: the drag handle shows on the column edge and dragging left makes the column wider.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
