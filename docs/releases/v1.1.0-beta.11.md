# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 11

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## Fixes

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 10, plus:

- **Accent colours are back.** In beta 10 four of the five accent dots in Settings → Appearance (and on first launch) showed no colour. Each dot shows its colour again.
- **The Settings side panel stays still.** In the desktop app the side panel no longer scrolls with the page; only the settings on the right scroll, and **Back to workspace** stays at the bottom.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and a PKG.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
