# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 2

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## What's new

- **A calmer look across the app.** Home, the sidebar, Tasks, Flashcards, Templates, Graph, Pages table, Files, Settings and Workspaces share one design system: quiet surfaces, one accent colour, plain labels and menus, and radii that follow the Corner radius setting.
- **Pages.** A new page header (icon, title, properties, cover), ten built-in covers plus your own colours or image, many more page icons, a flatter formatting bar, a redesigned "/" menu, and callouts, code blocks, tables, toggles and quotes that match.
- **Tables** keep a steady frame and direction when text switches between Persian and English, open their menu on click and close it on the next click, and follow the corner radius.
- **Databases.** Every view (table, board, list, gallery, calendar, timeline, chart, map, feed, form, dashboard) follows the same style. Tags, statuses and option labels look the same everywhere.
- **Graph.** A full-page map with a side card for the selected page. Relations, dependencies and sub-items can be shown on their own or together once a database has them.
- **Back and forward** between the pages and views you visited (toolbar buttons, Alt + arrows, mouse side buttons), with your scroll position restored.
- **Drag and drop.** The destination chooser opens right under the pointer. Blank space in a code block or table is the grip for moving it.

## Fixes

- Edits made shortly before leaving a page, or after a background reload, could come back as an older version when you returned to the page. Fixed.
- Bullet markers no longer hang out of a column when a list mixes Persian and English.
- The Table cell title no longer grows large in reading mode.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup or MSI installer.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
