# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 14

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## New

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 13, plus:

- **Pick a page with one click, open it with two.** In the side panel a single click selects a page, shown in the accent colour, and a double-click opens it. Folders still open and close with one click.
- **Reveal the open note with Ctrl+Alt+R** (⌘⌥R on a Mac). It finds the note in the side panel, opening the panel if it is hidden. You can change the key in Settings → Shortcuts.

## Fixes

- **The side panel button is in the top bar.** The button that shows and hides the side panel sits before the back and forward arrows, instead of on the icon rail or in the panel's heading. Ctrl+B still works.
- **The icon rail keeps its shape.** Opening or folding the icon rail no longer moves or reshapes its icons; it widens smoothly and the labels fade in. With Reduce motion on, it opens at once.
- **Reader mode keeps your place and your width.** Entering or leaving reader mode stays on the part you were reading, and the page keeps the same width in both modes.
- **Cleaner printing and PDFs.** Pages print in the light theme on A4, diagrams included, with only the title and the content: no side panel, window bar, toolbars or page properties.
- **The block handle stays clear of the bars.** The six-dot handle no longer sits over the top bar or the formatting toolbar, and it stands on the left, or on the right when the whole app is in Persian.
- **Show as diagram only on diagrams.** The button appears only on Mermaid code, not on every code block.
- **Diagram errors stay readable.** When a diagram cannot be drawn, its buttons sit above the message instead of over it.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and a PKG.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
