# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 14

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## Fixes

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 13, plus:

- **The side panel button is in the top bar.** The button that shows and hides the side panel now sits before the back and forward arrows, instead of on the icon rail or in the panel's heading. Ctrl+B still works.
- **The icon rail keeps its shape.** Opening or folding the icon rail no longer moves or reshapes its icons; only the labels appear and disappear.
- **A calmer opening.** The icon rail now widens and fades its labels in smoothly instead of jumping. With Reduce motion on, it opens at once.

## Downloads

- Windows: EXE setup.
- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and a PKG.

## Existing workspaces

Your existing vaults, notes, attachments, and settings remain compatible.
