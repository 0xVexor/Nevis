# Nevis 1.1.0 beta 17

A pre-release for testing. It is not offered as an update unless **Receive pre-release updates** is turned on in Settings → About → Updates.

## New

Everything in 1.1.0 beta 16, plus:

- **Smoother block moving.**
  - Drop a block on the left or right part of another block to put them side by side; the pointer no longer has to reach the edge. The middle of a block and the margin beside the text still just reorder, so dragging straight down from the handle never makes columns by accident.
  - Once a block is shown side by side, a small drift back toward the middle keeps it there instead of flickering between the two.
  - The blocks below the drop point slide apart to open a gap, on the page and inside columns.
  - On release the block slides from where it was to where it lands; a cancelled move sends its card back to the block.
  - The card under the pointer stays closer to it, and the preview fades between a line and a column layout.
  - Near the top or bottom of the page, scrolling starts gently and speeds up the closer the pointer gets to the edge.
- **PDFs with a header and footer.** Every printed page after the first shows the note's title and the date at the top and the page number at the bottom, in the app's typeface. On macOS the print engine may leave them out.

## Fixes

- **One highlighted row in the pages panel.** A row under the pointer is only greyed; the accent colour is kept for the selected page.
- **A clearer pages panel header.** The panel's title is always shown in full, and the header no longer has its own hide button; the panel closes from the toggle in the top bar or its shortcut.
- **The icon rail stays still.** Settings, Files, Trash, New and the workspace no longer shift when the rail opens or closes.
- **Titles in the top bar are no longer cut.** Letters that reach below the line, as in Persian titles, show in full.
- **The drop preview is drawn whole** beside the first and last blocks of a page.

- macOS: universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and a PKG.
