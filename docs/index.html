Nevis.

Nevis is a local-first notes workspace for Windows and macOS. Your pages, attachments and flashcards live in a plain folder on your own computer. Optional sync keeps that folder in step across your devices through a Nevis sync server (a user name and a password; no email address).

  • Rich-text pages with links, backlinks and a graph view
  • Spaced-repetition flashcards
  • Persian and English, right-to-left friendly
  • Your data stays yours: open, readable files; no account with us
